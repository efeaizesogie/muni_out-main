Town of Paradise Muni Resource Guide

This comprehensive guide will walk you through the essential processes of creating events, publishing news posts, and managing account settings. Whether you're organizing community gatherings, sharing important updates, or maintaining team access controls, these step-by-step instructions will help you navigate the platform efficiently.

Each section provides detailed information to ensure you can make the most of the app's features while keeping your content organized and your team structure well-maintained.

How to Create an Event
  1. From the main dashboard, click "Events" under the "Communications" section in the left sidebar menu
  1. Click the blue "+ Add Event" button in the top-right corner of the screen
  1. In the "Create Event" form that appears, fill out the following information:
    • Click "Upload Event Post Image" to add a featured image for your event
    • Select an event category from the dropdown menu
    • Enter the event name in the "Event Name" field
    • Provide the venue name in the "Venue" field
    • Write an event description using the text editor (you have options for bold, italic, underline, and different list formats)
    • If applicable, enter a Register URL (optional) - this will create a Register button linking to the provided URL
    • Set the Event Date & Time using the date picker and time selection dropdowns
  1. Once all information is entered:
    • Click "Reset" to clear all fields and start over, or
    • Click "Submit" to publish your event

After submission, your event will appear in the Events section of the app, where users can view it. Events can be sorted by "Most Recent" and searched by name using the search bar at the top of the events list.

How to Create a News Post
  1. From the main dashboard, navigate to the "News" section in the left sidebar menu under "Communications"
  1. Click the blue "+ Create News Post" button in the top-right corner of the screen
  1. In the "Create News Post" form that appears, follow these steps:
    • Click the "Upload News Post Image" button to add a featured image
    • Enter your news title in the "News Title" field
    • Toggle the "External Link" option if needed
    • Use the text editor toolbar to format your post content (you have options for bold, italic, underline, and different list formats)
    • Type your news post content in the main text area
  1. Once you've completed your post:
    • Click "Reset" to clear all fields and start over, or
    • Click "Submit" to publish your news post

After submission, your news post will appear in the main news section of the app, where users can view it. Posts are sorted by date, with a "Most Recent" filter option available.

Managing the Account Settings
  1. Accessing Account Settings
    • Click your profile icon in the top-right corner
    • Select "Account Settings" from the dropdown menu
    • You'll see four main tabs: Personal, Team, Notifications, and Access Control
  1. Personal Information Management
  • Under the "Personal" tab, you can update:
    • Your name and contact information
    • Profile picture (supports PNGs and JPEGs under 10MB)
    • Password (requires current and new password)
  • Click "Update" to save any changes
  1. Team Management
    • Navigate to the "Team" tab to:
      • View all team members and their last active status
      • Create groups using the "Create Group" button
      • Add new accounts using the "Add Account" button
      • Organize team members into groups
      • Manage existing team members and their roles
  1. Access Control
    • Under the "Access Control" tab:
      • View and manage different account types
      • Set permissions for various account roles
      • Add new account types as needed
      • Customize access levels for different groups and roles
  1. Adding New Team Members
    • Click "Add Account" button
    • Fill in required information:
      • Personal details (name, phone, email)
      • Select appropriate account role
      • Choose access type
      • Assign to specific groups if applicable
  1. Creating and Managing Groups
    • Use "Create Group" to set up new teams
    • Name the group
    • Select members to add
    • Manage group permissions and access level

    Key Tips:

    • Always review access permissions when adding new team members
    • Regularly audit team member roles and permissions
    • Keep group structures organized for easier management
    • Update team member information as needed
